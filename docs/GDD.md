# Game Design Document — NeedleFactory

## High concept
NeedleFactory starts with the literal challenge of finding a needle hidden in clutter. Successful manual discovery funds tools and machinery until the player is operating and optimizing an increasingly sophisticated needle-recovery factory.

The game should evolve from tactile search into automation without making the original act of finding a needle irrelevant.

## Core loop
**Search → extract → inspect → process → package → sell → improve → unlock harder material streams.**

Manual skill creates bursts of value; automation creates sustained value. Neither should completely invalidate the other.

## Experience goals
The player should feel:
1. curiosity while searching;
2. tactile satisfaction when manipulating material;
3. a clear payoff when a needle is discovered;
4. anticipation while buying a machine;
5. satisfaction watching machines physically cooperate;
6. optimization pressure from bottlenecks;
7. visible growth from a small workbench to a complex factory.

## Progression sketch
Early: hand search, tray, simple magnet.
Mid: vibration table, conveyors, magnetic separator, scanner, robotic picker, packaging.
Late: parallel lines, specialized material handling, quality control, research, contracts and advanced automation.

Potential environments/material streams: hay, sawdust, textile scraps, mixed waste, electronic scrap and industrial material. These are progression concepts, not yet final content.

## Anti-clicker principles
- Avoid progression based primarily on repetitive tapping.
- New purchases should frequently change behavior, layout or strategy.
- Bottlenecks should matter.
- Machines should have tradeoffs and interactions.
- Upgrades should include qualitative unlocks, not only percentage increases.
- The player must be able to understand why production improved or stalled.

## Scope discipline
Narrative is light environmental flavor, not a multi-day story campaign. The factory and its evolution are the main character.

Detailed economy numbers and content counts remain intentionally unset until prototypes provide measurements.
