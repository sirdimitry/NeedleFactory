### Economy / product tunables

`EconomyConfig.products()` holds all first-step values/prices; FactoryConfig
holds patch count, receiver/chamber cadence and chamber capacity. No UI-defined
economic truth, external dependency or offline clock.

| Item | Current tuning |
|---|---:|
| Full loose straw lot | 6 credits |
| Same full lot after binding | 16 credits |
| Manually secured raw needle | 3 credits each |
| Feed rake | 28 credits |
| Feed guides (optional) | 16 credits, requires rake |
| Straw Baler | 42 credits, requires rake; guides optional |
| Receiver transit | 0.55 s |
| Chamber cycle / capacity | 1.1 s / 12 patch units |
| Product lot | 120 normalized patch units |

Reward = `3*securedNeedles + floor((6*looseUnits + 16*bundledUnits)/120)`.
Round the aggregate once; a unit cannot be sold in both grades. No arbitrary
perfect cash grant. Full 3/3 raw lot earns **15**; full bundled lot earns **25**.
Finish is available after at least one saleable credit, even straw-only. Preview
is a lower bound; accepted chamber work may finish during drain. Unfed straw and
hidden needles are discarded without payment; no already secured value is removed.
Buying Baler after results never upgrades the previously sold loose receipt.

Typical design estimate remains 10–13 per raw batch, rake after 2–3 good batches,
and Baler around batch 7–9 with optional guides (~7–10 minutes at the target human
cadence). Perfect GL runs bought rake after 2, guides after 3 and Baler after 6.
Without guides, five perfect lots can afford rake+Baler. These are arithmetic
estimates, **not measured human timing**. The scripted full-drain verification
deliberately searched leftwards first and took 116–132 simulated seconds per lot;
it does not establish the desired 45–90-second first-party experience. Owner must
judge search/cleanup workload and timing before any further price tuning.
