Barlow-derived NeedlePanel bitmap typography is baked offline and OFL-licensed;
source/license/reproduction notes are in `assets/source/ui/README.md`.

**Stylized 2.5D miniature industrial diorama.**

The factory should feel tangible, readable and slightly playful rather than photorealistic or toy-like in a childish way.

## Visual principles
- Strong silhouettes for every machine.
- Clear material differentiation: metal, rubber, plastic, glass, textile, organic clutter.
- Restrained industrial base palette with purposeful accent colors for state and interaction.
- Soft dimensional lighting and contact shadows.
- Motion communicates function: belts travel, rollers rotate, magnets react, gates switch, robotic tools physically act.
- Important needles must remain discoverable without becoming visually obvious.
- UI should feel integrated with the industrial world but remain highly legible.

## Camera
Prefer a controlled 2.5D/isometric-ish presentation that gives depth without requiring an expensive unrestricted 3D world. Exact projection is to be validated by prototype.

## Asset pipeline
Prototype geometry/sprites must be replaceable. Establish naming, source-file ownership, export resolution, atlas policy and animation conventions before producing a large asset library.

## Effects
Use particles, screen motion, audio and haptics as feedback, not camouflage. Discovery of a needle should feel excellent without excessive visual noise.

## Accessibility
Do not communicate machine state exclusively through color. Preserve readable contrast and scalable UI. Avoid mandatory rapid repeated tapping.

This document is a starting direction; visual production requires explicit owner approval after prototype comparisons.
