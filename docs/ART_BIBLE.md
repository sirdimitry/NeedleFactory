# Art Bible — Draft

## Stage 2 prototype material contract
Latest owner feedback accepts the 1.3-metre needle length and current visual
width (+1.25 actual pixels over the thin physical shaft; no further thickening).
Keep subdued metal during early search, then reward substantial exposure with
a cool polished-steel tint and two nested internal specular lobes with vertex falloff.
Attitude-driven internal reflection is completely off at <=60% straw-uncovered
visibility; smooth gain reaches 0.32 at 75%, 0.70 at 90%, and 1.0 only at full
exposure. Both tint and reflection use this gate. No external glow/bloom or
through-cover marker. This supersedes historical thinning-only guidance below.
Latest owner clarification, 2026-10-03: the needle stays at the Stage 1 length
of 1.3 metres. Make it thinner with a restrained metallic-grey shaft, a sharp
tip and a small eye; no white stripe, glow, enlarged eye or length pulse.
Straw uses longer, thinner tapered ribbons with smooth large-radius quadratic
bends. Twelve individually varied crossing straws share one physical patch, retaining dense initial tray
coverage without thousands of rigid bodies. These procedural shapes remain
replaceable prototype art. Both orientations must expose the whole machine
chain through separately composed layouts.
The final-feedback pass uses 320 patches / 3,840 visible straws, irregular
dense pockets and varied peripheral coverage. Local strand angles, bends, lengths,
spacing and tones differ; a comb of seven parallel strands is no longer used.
Needle shaft width is 0.018 metres; its 1.3-metre length is unchanged.
Landscape is a compact two-level return-flow workshop with a side instrument
console. Toggles remain 78 × 60 UI units and never grow with screen width.
Latest responsive contract: search tray is 248 × 198.4 units in BOTH orientations,
machine unit is 1, and FactoryCanvas calibrates the same device baseline before
authored anchors. On Samsung: 2.7 pixels per unit in both orientations. Portrait
retains its scale; landscape changes belt length and branch routing rather than
shrinking modules. Landscape identity/counters live under the search tray and
controls in a separate side console, leaving full height for the factory.
Individual-strand rotated bounds allow coverage near the tray edges without
oversized invisible margins. Crossing angles stay varied on the belt; shallow
perspective compresses each loose load to the belt bed instead of forming a brush.
Magnet housing spans the live belt, buffer is a folded hopper with a pneumatic
stop, and the packer has a drive housing, feed rollers, jaws and open ports.

## Direction
### Stage 2 visual polish construction
The prototype uses an authored shallow cabinet projection: top planes,
consistent downward front-face extrusion and contact shadows offset right/down.
The complete line is anchored to one inset workshop plinth. Teal-painted steel,
warm grey deck, dark rubber, brushed steel, copper windings and ochre cardboard
share a restrained palette; bevels/rounded cast housings replace blunt blocks.

The full-height feed apron connects the table to the end drum. Belt frame, return
depth, bearings, drive gearbox, support trestles, side rails and directional ribs
are built together. Separator yoke/coil mounts to a gantry joining both lanes.
Bolted collar mouths connect lined metal chutes, buffer, packer and output.
Wrapper roll, jaws and exit path are visible. Separate discharge chutes meet
the residue/missed trays at the exact animated material endpoints.

Full-window dark background extends under the system bars and cutout; only
important content avoids their safe insets. No padded native SurfaceView or
light Activity background. UI is a workshop instrument console: four grouped counters, Packaged emphasis,
compact touch controls, explicit ON/OFF switches, a primary Run/Pause action
and a gated Next batch action. Feed gate controls the hand-fed mouth; the default
hint explicitly asks the player to push right. Telemetry is an optional drawer inside reserved
console space. Labels occupy machine front nameplates rather than material.
Barlow-derived NeedlePanel bitmap typography is baked offline and OFL-licensed;
source/license/reproduction notes are in `assets/source/ui/README.md`.

**Stylized 2.5D miniature industrial diorama.**

The factory should feel tangible, readable and slightly playful rather than photorealistic or toy-like in a childish way.

## Visual principles
- Strong silhouettes for every machine.
- Clear material differentiation: metal, rubber, plastic, glass, textile, organic clutter.
- Restrained industrial base palette with purposeful accent colors for state and interaction.
- Soft dimensional lighting and contact shadows.
- Motion communicates function: belts travel, rollers rotate, magnets react, gates switch, robotic tools physically act.
- Important needles must remain discoverable without becoming visually obvious.
- UI should feel integrated with the industrial world but remain highly legible.

## Camera
Prefer a controlled 2.5D/isometric-ish presentation that gives depth without requiring an expensive unrestricted 3D world. Exact projection is to be validated by prototype.

## Asset pipeline
Prototype geometry/sprites must be replaceable. Establish naming, source-file ownership, export resolution, atlas policy and animation conventions before producing a large asset library.

## Effects
Use particles, screen motion, audio and haptics as feedback, not camouflage. Discovery of a needle should feel excellent without excessive visual noise.

## Accessibility
Do not communicate machine state exclusively through color. Preserve readable contrast and scalable UI. Avoid mandatory rapid repeated tapping.

This document is a starting direction; visual production requires explicit owner approval after prototype comparisons.
